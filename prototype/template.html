Meetle

Find one word that sits between two others in meaning. Three pairs a day, three guesses each.

and

    How scoring works

    Every word in the list has 100 numbers describing how it’s used, learned from a large body of text. Two words with similar numbers mean similar things.

    A guess is measured against both words, and only the weaker of the two counts, so a word that’s great for one side and useless for the other scores low. Very general words like “area” or “thing” get a small penalty, since they’re loosely close to everything.

    Your guess is then ranked against 12,000 common words. The top word scores 100; the top 10 score about 70 or more. Your best guess in each pair counts.

    On the map, the peak is the best possible word. Higher is better, and a dot that drifts left or right leans toward that word.
